Schedule rows

Newest date wins

The display uses the row with the latest date that is on or before today. Most of the time you only need one row and simply edit it.

Advanced: schedule a future change

Add a second row that takes effect from a chosen date (e.g. the first day of Ramadan). Until then the current row stays in effect.

The new row starts as a copy of the schedule you’re editing; edit it afterwards.

Live preview

portrait

The preview shows the display as it is right now (saved settings). Save, then refresh to see your changes.

How this works

  • Each setting has a built-in default. Settings you change are written to the Sheet; “Use default” removes the row so the display goes back to its factory value.
  • Adhan times are never edited here — they always come from AlAdhan (ISNA) for Regina.
  • Iqamah times live in the Iqamah schedule, announcements in Announcements.
